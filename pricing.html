A few honest pricing notes.

Some technology problems are simple. Some become more complicated once we see the device, account, network, or setup. If the work looks bigger than expected, I will pause and explain the options before continuing so you can decide what makes sense.

Hardware, replacement parts, subscriptions, apps, cables, accessories, and paid software are not included unless specifically stated.

What I do not offer.

Friendly Tech Concierge focuses on setup, troubleshooting, education, and everyday home technology help. To keep the service safe and reliable, I do not provide:

  • Phone screen repair
  • Laptop screen replacement
  • Keyboard replacements
  • Board-level repair or soldering
  • Complex hardware rebuilds

Common pricing questions.

What if I choose the wrong service?

That is completely fine. Tell me what is happening in normal words, and I will help match the request to the right service before confirming the appointment.

Will I need to share my passwords?

No. Whenever possible, you enter your own passwords. If account access is needed, I explain why first and ask permission.

Can you help my parent or loved one?

Yes. With their permission, I can help a parent or loved one in the Las Vegas area and provide a simple update after the visit.

What if the issue takes longer than expected?

I pause, explain what I found, and discuss options before doing extra work. The goal is no surprises and no pressure.
