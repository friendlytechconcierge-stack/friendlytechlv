Important scope note.

Friendly Tech Concierge focuses on practical in-home setup, troubleshooting, and education. I do not provide phone screen repair, laptop screen replacement, board-level repair, soldering, or complex hardware repair.

Which option should I choose?

I do not know what is wrong.

Choose the $75 in-home tech support visit and describe what is happening. I will confirm whether it sounds like a good fit before scheduling.

I am worried it might be a scam.

Stop interacting with the pop-up, email, call, or message. Do not give remote access or payment information. Request help and describe what you saw.

I am booking for a parent or loved one.

Use the request form and include the customer’s name, location, device, problem, and preferred contact method. With permission, I can share a simple visit summary afterward.

Do you charge if the issue is outside your scope?

I try to confirm fit before scheduling. If the issue is clearly outside my scope, I will say so and avoid wasting your time.

Ready to request help?

Tell me what is happening in normal words. I will help you choose the right service and confirm the next step before the visit.
