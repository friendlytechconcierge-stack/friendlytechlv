How we can help

Practical help with everyday technology.

Friendly Tech Concierge provides limited, appointment-based assistance with common consumer technology needs in the Las Vegas area. Availability and scope are determined after reviewing each request.

New Device Setup

Basic setup of new personal computers, tablets, printers, and similar consumer devices. This may include initial settings, updates, software setup, account sign-in, and connecting compatible peripherals.

Everyday Tech Help

Help with common technology problems such as software difficulties, email setup, device settings, printer connections, and basic computer troubleshooting.

Technology Guidance

Plain-language help understanding a device, application, online service, or technology choice. This can include basic instruction, comparing consumer options, and learning everyday features.

Home Device Setup

Basic connection and configuration of consumer devices when the work does not involve wiring, permanent installation, structural modification, electrical work, alarm or surveillance installation, or another specialized service.

Before assistance is confirmed

Every request is reviewed individually. Some requests require a different provider, manufacturer support, specialized credentials, or services we do not offer.

Friendly Tech Concierge does not provide:

  • Electrical work or structured cabling
  • Alarm, surveillance, or security-camera installation
  • Commercial networking, servers, or managed IT
  • Business cybersecurity services
  • Guaranteed account or data recovery
  • Specialized hardware repair, soldering, or board-level repair
  • Work requiring contractor licensing, permits, or specialized certification
  • Emergency or around-the-clock support
