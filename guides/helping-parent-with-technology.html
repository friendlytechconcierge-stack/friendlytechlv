Helping a Parent With Technology?

What to gather before booking help for a parent, grandparent, or loved one in the Las Vegas area.

Estimated time: 5–10 minutesDifficulty: BeginnerPlain-English guide

Before you start

If there is a possible scam, bank issue, or remote access concern, act quickly and avoid further account sign-ins until the situation is reviewed.

1

Write down the main problem

Ask what is not working in normal words. A short description is enough.

2

List the devices involved

Note whether the issue involves a phone, tablet, computer, printer, Wi-Fi, TV, or account.

3

Confirm permission

Make sure your parent or loved one is comfortable having someone help in the home.

4

Gather account basics safely

Do not text passwords. Instead, make sure they know where passwords are stored or can enter them during the visit.

5

Decide what update you want

With the customer’s permission, a short visit summary can help the family understand what was fixed and what still needs attention.

When to ask for help

If the issue still is not resolved, do not keep guessing. Write down what happened, what you tried, and any error messages you saw. Friendly Tech Concierge can help you sort through it calmly in your home.

Still stuck?
