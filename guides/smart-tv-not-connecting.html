Smart TV Help Guide

Roku, Fire Stick, or Smart TV Not Connecting?

What to check when streaming apps, Wi-Fi, remotes, sign-ins, or TV settings stop cooperating.

Estimated time: 5–10 minutesDifficulty: BeginnerPlain-English guide

Before you start

Stop if a screen asks you to call a support number or pay a fee to activate a device. That can be a scam.

1

Restart the TV and device

Turn the TV off and unplug the Roku, Fire Stick, or streaming device for 30 seconds, then reconnect it.

2

Check the right input

Make sure the TV is on the correct HDMI input. Many streaming devices are fine but hidden on the wrong input.

3

Test Wi-Fi

Open a streaming app or network setting to confirm the TV or streaming device is connected to Wi-Fi.

4

Check subscriptions and passwords

Streaming problems are often account-related. Make sure the subscription is active and the password works.

5

Replace batteries or re-pair the remote

If the remote is not responding, try fresh batteries or the pairing button if your remote has one.

When to ask for help

If the issue still is not resolved, do not keep guessing. Write down what happened, what you tried, and any error messages you saw. Friendly Tech Concierge can help you sort through it calmly in your home.
