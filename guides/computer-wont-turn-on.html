Computer Help Guide

Computer Won’t Turn On?

Try these safe checks before assuming the computer is broken or your files are gone.

Estimated time: 5–10 minutesDifficulty: BeginnerPlain-English guide

Before you start

Stop if you smell burning, see sparks, notice liquid damage, or hear unusual popping sounds. If important files or passwords are on the computer, get help before trying random fixes.

1

Check the power cable

Make sure the power cable is firmly connected to the computer and the wall. If it is a laptop, check both ends of the charger and the charging brick.

2

Try a different outlet

Plug the computer or charger into another outlet that you know works. If it is plugged into a power strip, try the wall outlet directly.

3

Look for lights, sounds, or fan noise

Notice whether you see lights, hear a fan, hear beeping, or see the screen flash. These clues help narrow down whether it is power or display related.

4

Hold the power button for 10 seconds

Press and hold the power button for about 10 seconds, wait a few seconds, then press it normally.

5

Unplug extra devices

Disconnect printers, USB drives, docking stations, and external drives. Then try starting the computer with only the power cable connected.

When to ask for help

If the issue still is not resolved, do not keep guessing. Write down what happened, what you tried, and any error messages you saw. Friendly Tech Concierge can help you sort through it calmly in your home.
