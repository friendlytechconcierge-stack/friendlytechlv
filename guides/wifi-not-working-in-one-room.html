Wi-Fi Not Working in One Room?

Understand weak signal, dead zones, router placement, and why some rooms connect better than others.

Estimated time: 5–10 minutesDifficulty: BeginnerPlain-English guide

Before you start

Stop if you need to change router settings and are not sure what they mean. Incorrect settings can disconnect other devices.

1

Test near the router

Stand close to the router and test the same device. If it works near the router but not in the room, signal strength may be the issue.

2

Check what is between rooms

Walls, mirrors, appliances, metal, and distance can weaken Wi-Fi. Bedrooms and offices far from the router often struggle.

3

Restart modem and router

Unplug the modem and router, wait 60 seconds, then reconnect them. Give the network a few minutes to return.

4

Check other devices

If every device has trouble in one room, it is likely a signal issue. If only one device struggles, that device may need attention.

5

Avoid buying random extenders first

Extenders can help, but the wrong one or bad placement can make Wi-Fi more confusing.

When to ask for help

If the issue still is not resolved, do not keep guessing. Write down what happened, what you tried, and any error messages you saw. Friendly Tech Concierge can help you sort through it calmly in your home.
