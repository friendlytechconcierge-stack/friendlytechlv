Email Help Guide

📧 I Can’t Get Into My Email — What Should I Try?

A step-by-step guide for email login problems, missing mail, password prompts, app issues, and suspicious messages.

Estimated time: 10–15 minutes Difficulty: Beginner Easy to share

First, take a breath.

Email is connected to almost everything: bills, banking, shopping, passwords, appointments, travel, photos, and family messages. When email stops working, it can feel urgent.

This guide helps you figure out whether the problem is your password, the email app, the internet connection, storage, or a security issue.

Before you try to fix email

  • Do not give your email password or verification code to someone who calls you.
  • Do not click links in suspicious emails claiming your account will be closed.
  • Do not keep guessing passwords if you are close to being locked out.
  • If you think someone else accessed your email, avoid using that same device for sensitive password changes until it is checked.

Start with these simple checks

Go one step at a time. You do not need to understand every technical detail. The goal is to safely narrow down what might be happening.

1

Check internet first

Open a normal website. If nothing loads, email may not be the problem. Fix Wi-Fi or internet first.

2

Try email in a web browser

If the email app on your phone is not working, try signing in through the official website. This helps separate an app problem from an account problem.

3

Look carefully at the error message

Does it say wrong password, cannot connect to server, account locked, storage full, or something else? The exact wording matters.

4

Check whether the password changed

If the password was changed on one device, other devices may keep asking for the old password until they are updated.

5

Check storage or mailbox full warnings

Some email accounts stop receiving mail when storage is full. Look for warnings about storage, mailbox quota, or account space.

6

Check spam, junk, and filters

Missing emails may be in Spam, Junk, Trash, Archive, or filtered into another folder. Search by sender name if you know it.

7

Be careful with “account closing” emails

Scammers often send fake warnings that your email will be closed unless you click a link. Go directly to the official email website instead.

Common reasons this happens

  • Wrong or changed password
  • Email app needs sign-in again
  • Internet connection issue
  • Mailbox storage full
  • Email going to spam or junk
  • Account locked for security
  • Suspicious phishing email

What not to do

  • Do not click suspicious “verify your account” links.
  • Do not share verification codes.
  • Do not delete large amounts of email without understanding what you are deleting.
  • Do not assume email is gone just because it is not in the inbox.
