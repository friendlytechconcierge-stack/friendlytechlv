Online Safety Guide

Is This a Tech Support Scam?

Warning signs before calling a number on a pop-up, giving remote access, buying gift cards, or sharing account information.

Estimated time: 5–10 minutesDifficulty: BeginnerPlain-English guide

Before you start

If you already gave access, shared a code, or paid money, stop using the device for sensitive accounts and get help reviewing what happened.

1

Be suspicious of urgent popups

A popup that says your computer is infected and tells you to call immediately is a major warning sign.

2

Do not call numbers from popups

Real companies rarely use scary popups with phone numbers. Close the browser or shut down the computer if needed.

3

Do not buy gift cards

Legitimate tech support, banks, and government agencies do not ask for gift cards as payment.

4

Do not give remote access quickly

Remote access lets someone control your computer. Only allow it when you know and trust the person helping you.

5

Pause before sharing codes

Verification codes can give someone access to your accounts. Do not read codes to unexpected callers.

When to ask for help

If the issue still is not resolved, do not keep guessing. Write down what happened, what you tried, and any error messages you saw. Friendly Tech Concierge can help you sort through it calmly in your home.
