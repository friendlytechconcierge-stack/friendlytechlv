Computer Help Guide

Slow Computer?

What to check before replacing your computer, paying for unnecessary upgrades, or losing more time fighting with it.

Estimated time: 5–10 minutesDifficulty: BeginnerPlain-English guide

Before you start

Stop if the computer is extremely slow after a suspicious popup, support call, or download. It may be safer to review it before signing into important accounts.

1

Restart first

A restart can clear stuck updates, frozen programs, and memory issues. Shut down fully, wait 30 seconds, and start again.

2

Check for updates

Windows or macOS updates can slow things down while they are pending. Make sure updates have finished before judging the computer.

3

Close extra programs

Too many open tabs and apps can make a good computer feel old. Close what you are not using and test again.

4

Check storage space

A computer with very little free space can become slow. Photos, videos, downloads, and duplicate files are common causes.

5

Watch for popups

Frequent browser popups, fake warnings, or unexpected toolbars can point to adware or browser problems.

When to ask for help

If the issue still is not resolved, do not keep guessing. Write down what happened, what you tried, and any error messages you saw. Friendly Tech Concierge can help you sort through it calmly in your home.
