Phone & Tablet Help Guide

New Phone Setup Checklist

A simple checklist for contacts, photos, email, apps, passwords, updates, and safety settings.

Estimated time: 5–10 minutesDifficulty: BeginnerPlain-English guide

Before you start

Do not erase the old phone until you are sure the new phone has the information and accounts you need.

1

Back up the old phone

Before switching, confirm contacts, photos, messages, and important apps are backed up.

2

Move contacts and photos

Check that contacts and recent photos appear on the new phone before erasing or trading the old one.

3

Set up email and key apps

Make sure email, banking, medical, rideshare, password, and communication apps are working.

4

Check verification codes

Many accounts use text messages or authenticator apps. Confirm you can receive sign-in codes on the new phone.

5

Adjust safety settings

Set up screen lock, emergency contacts, Find My Device or Find My iPhone, and backup settings.

When to ask for help

If the issue still is not resolved, do not keep guessing. Write down what happened, what you tried, and any error messages you saw. Friendly Tech Concierge can help you sort through it calmly in your home.
