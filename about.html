Why I started Friendly Tech Concierge.

Technology is supposed to make life easier, but for many people it does the opposite.

I’ve met people who avoid using devices because they’re afraid of making a mistake.

I’ve met seniors who feel left behind as technology changes.

I’ve met families who spend hours trying to solve problems that should take minutes.

Friendly Tech Concierge was created to bridge that gap.

My mission is to provide professional technology support that is approachable, patient, and focused on helping people feel empowered rather than overwhelmed.

What I want customers to feel.

When I visit your home, my goal isn’t just to solve the problem.

It’s to make sure you understand what happened, know what your options are, and feel comfortable asking questions.

Friendly Tech Concierge was created to help people feel supported, not embarrassed. Empowered, not overwhelmed.

Services intentionally not offered.

Friendly Tech Concierge focuses on practical in-home support, setup, troubleshooting, and education. To keep service safe, focused, and reliable, the business does not provide:

  • Phone screen repair
  • Laptop screen replacement
  • Keyboard replacements
  • Board-level repair or soldering
  • Complex hardware rebuilds
