Why I started Friendly Tech Concierge.

Technology support should feel helpful, respectful, and easy to understand. Too often, it does not.

I have seen people avoid asking for help because they are afraid of looking foolish. I have seen families lose hours trying to solve simple tech problems. I have seen seniors feel left behind when phones, apps, passwords, and online accounts change faster than anyone explains them.

Friendly Tech Concierge was created for those moments.

My goal is to give you a calm, trustworthy person to call when technology gets frustrating, confusing, or overwhelming.

The service is built around one simple idea:

You should not have to feel embarrassed just because technology is not working.

I will meet you where you are, explain what I am doing, and help you feel more comfortable using the technology already in your home.

What makes this different.

The visit should feel more personal than a repair ticket and more useful than someone quickly fixing the issue without explaining anything.

1

I listen first

You can describe the problem in normal words. I will ask questions, confirm what you need, and explain the likely next step.

2

I explain as I go

I will tell you what I am checking and why, without overwhelming you with unnecessary technical details.

3

I leave things clearer

When the visit is finished, the goal is for you to understand what was done and what to do next if the issue returns.

For families helping from afar.

If you are trying to help a parent, grandparent, or loved one with technology from another city, I know how stressful that can be.

With the customer’s permission, I can share a simple visit summary so everyone understands what was fixed, what still needs attention, and what I recommend next.

Services intentionally not offered.

Friendly Tech Concierge focuses on safe, practical in-home setup, troubleshooting, education, and everyday technology support. I do not provide:

  • Phone screen repair
  • Laptop screen replacement
  • Keyboard replacements
  • Board-level repair or soldering
  • Complex hardware rebuilds
