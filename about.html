About Friendly Tech Concierge

Everyday technology, explained clearly.

A local technology assistance service created to make everyday technology easier to understand and use.

A patient, practical approach.

Friendly Tech Concierge provides straightforward assistance with common personal computers, devices, software, and everyday technology questions. Service is offered on a limited, appointment-based basis in the Las Vegas area.

Our approach is simple: listen carefully, explain things clearly, and determine whether a request fits the services we currently provide.

Intentionally focused

We concentrate on basic consumer technology assistance. Requests involving licensed, structural, electrical, commercial, security-system, or specialized repair work are outside our scope.
