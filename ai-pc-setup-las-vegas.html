What is an AI PC?

An AI PC is a newer Windows computer with a special chip that helps with certain AI features. You do not need to be an expert to use one. On some computers, this can improve features such as video-call effects, live captions, image tools, search, and other Windows features that use AI.

An AI PC may make sense when:

  • Your current computer is already old, slow, or no longer supported
  • You want a computer that should stay useful for several years
  • You regularly use video calls, creative tools, accessibility features, or newer Windows features
  • You are already buying a new computer and the price difference is reasonable

You may not need one when:

  • Your current computer still handles email, web browsing, documents, and streaming well
  • You are only considering an upgrade because “AI” sounds new
  • The AI model costs much more than a reliable standard Windows computer
  • You would rather keep your current computer and improve its setup first

Questions people usually ask before moving to a new computer.

“Will I lose my files?”

The goal is to identify and copy the important accessible files before the old computer is retired. No transfer method can promise that every hidden, damaged, encrypted, or inaccessible file will move, so the old computer should not be erased or discarded until you have reviewed the new setup.

“What happens to my old computer?”

You can keep it temporarily as a backup, repurpose it, donate it, recycle it, or prepare it for another person. Before giving it away, personal data should be backed up and the device should be securely reset. This can be discussed after the migration is confirmed.

“How long does it take?”

A basic new computer setup may take a few hours. Large photo libraries, slow old computers, slow internet, many programs, Windows updates, multiple email accounts, or printer problems can take longer. You will receive an expected scope and price before work begins.
