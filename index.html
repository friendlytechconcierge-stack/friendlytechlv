How the visit works.

The process is simple on purpose. You do not have to diagnose the issue first.

1

Tell me what is happening

Call, text, email, or use the request form. A normal description is enough.

2

I ask a few questions

I confirm the device, the issue, your location, preferred contact method, and expected price range.

3

We schedule the visit

If it is a good fit, we choose a time and I tell you what to have ready before I arrive.
