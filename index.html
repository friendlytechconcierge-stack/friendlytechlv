Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions before scheduling an in-home tech visit.

Do you come to my home?

Yes. Friendly Tech LV provides in-home technology support in the Las Vegas Valley service area.

Can family members schedule for a parent?

Yes. Adult children and family members can call or text to help arrange support for a loved one.

Do you help with iPhones, Android phones, tablets, Macs, and PCs?

Yes. We help with common setup, troubleshooting, basic training, and everyday use across many personal devices.

Can you help with passwords?

Yes, we can guide password resets and account recovery steps. For your safety, you should never share private passwords by text or email.

Do you repair broken phone or laptop screens?

No. Friendly Tech LV focuses on setup, troubleshooting, education, software support, connectivity, and everyday technology help.

What if the issue cannot be fixed during the visit?

We will explain what was found, recommend next steps, and help you understand whether a replacement, provider support, or specialist repair is needed.
